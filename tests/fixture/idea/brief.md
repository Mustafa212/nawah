# Idea Brief — Qahwa Points
Date: 2026-10-16    Mode: new    Audience: investor

> Test fixture. The idea is invented for testing nawah.

**One-liner:** For independent coffee shops in Cairo that lose regulars to the chains, Qahwa Points is a shared loyalty app that rewards visits across a network of shops; unlike paper stamp cards, it brings customers back to any member shop.

## Problem
Independent coffee shops in Cairo lose repeat customers to chains whose apps reward every visit. Paper stamp cards get lost and only work in one shop; owners have no data on who comes back.

## Customer segments and beachhead
- Independent coffee shops with 1–3 branches (payer).
- Their regular customers, 18–35, who visit several cafés a week (user).
- Beachhead: independent cafés in Zamalek and Maadi, about 150 shops within walking distance of each other.

## Jobs to be done
When a regular customer of mine starts going to a chain, I want a reason for them to come back that I can afford, so I can keep my daily sales steady.

## Current alternatives
Paper stamp cards; WhatsApp groups with offers; discounts at the counter; doing nothing.

## Solution
A customer app that collects points by scanning a QR code at the counter of any member shop, and a shop dashboard that shows returning customers and runs simple rewards.

## MVP scope
In: shop sign-up; QR stamps at the counter; customer points and rewards; a shop dashboard with visits per week.
Out: payments; delivery; promotions and push campaigns (v2); integration with point-of-sale systems.

## Why now
Smartphone use among the target customers is near universal, QR scanning became routine during 2020–2022, and the chains' apps have trained customers to expect rewards.

## Riskiest assumptions (ranked)
| # | Assumption | Type | Why it's risky |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Shops will pay a monthly fee for a shared program | viability | Owners are price-sensitive and used to free paper cards |
| 2 | Customers will install one more app | desirability | App fatigue; chains already have apps |
| 3 | Points across shops don't feel like helping a competitor | desirability | Owners may refuse to share customers |

## Success metric
30 shops stamping at least 100 visits a week by the end of month 3.
